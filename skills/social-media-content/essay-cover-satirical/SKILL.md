Create a hand-drawn satirical editorial cartoon on cream paper.

## SUBJECT

<Image subject goes here, written as a two-sentence max description of the scene.>

## VISUAL STYLE

* Graphite pencil on cream/off-white paper
* Traditional one-panel newspaper or magazine editorial cartoon
* Strong editorial caricature rather than realistic human anatomy
* Characters should look deliberately cartooned, not like normal people drawn in pencil
* Exaggerated head shapes, facial features, silhouettes, and body proportions
* Distinctive noses, chins, eyebrows, hairstyles, postures, and gestures
* Heads may be oversized relative to bodies
* Limbs and torsos may be simplified, stretched, compressed, or angular for comic effect
* Faces should be constructed from a few bold, expressive features rather than realistic detail
* Loose, confident, hand-drawn linework
* Visible pencil strokes and natural line variation
* Some doubled, overlapping, or imperfect lines
* Medium pencil weight, approximately HB to 2B
* Minimal rendering
* Strong, readable silhouettes
* Analog rather than digital appearance


The characters should immediately read as editorial-cartoon characters even without context.


Avoid realistic facial proportions, naturalistic anatomy, or portrait-like rendering.


## CARTOON LANGUAGE


Prioritize a clear visual idea over detailed illustration.


Use:


* strong character caricature
* exaggerated gestures and posture
* expressive facial construction
* visual irony
* simple exaggeration
* symbolic or metaphorical elements when appropriate
* dry observational humor
* economical composition


Push recognizable traits beyond normal proportions when it improves readability or humor.


A character's personality or role should be visible through silhouette, face shape, posture, and gesture before fine details are noticed.


The image should communicate its central situation quickly, with additional details rewarding a second look.


The humor should come from the situation, contradiction, exaggeration, or visual metaphor rather than slapstick.


## SHADING


Use restrained graphite shading.


* 2-3 values maximum: paper, mid-gray, dark graphite
* Simple hatching or occasional cross-hatching
* No smooth gradients
* Leave substantial areas of untouched paper
* Use darker values selectively for emphasis
* Keep shadows loose and sketched


Do not fully render every surface.


## COMPOSITION


* Square format
* 1080x1080px
* Main subject occupies approximately 60-70% of the frame
* Centered or slightly off-center composition
* Generous negative space
* Minimal environmental detail
* Include only props or background elements that help establish the situation or strengthen the joke
* Paper texture visible throughout
* Clear visual hierarchy
* Readable at thumbnail size


Avoid filling the frame simply because space is available.


## EXECUTION STYLE


Aim for the immediacy of a quick editorial sketch made by a skilled cartoonist.


* Confident mark-making
* Bold simplification of anatomy and features
* Deliberately distorted proportions
* Fewer strokes rather than more
* Slightly unfinished edges
* Natural construction marks may remain visible
* Loose anatomy is encouraged when it strengthens caricature
* Minimal erasing
* No unnecessary decorative detail
* Stop before the illustration feels polished or overworked


Aim for roughly 70% visual completion rather than a fully rendered drawing.


The imperfections and distortions should feel intentional and human.


## MOOD


Dry, intelligent, observational, mildly absurd.


The humor should be understated rather than loud.


The cartoon may critique human behavior, workplace habits, social conventions, leadership, communication, or everyday absurdity without becoming preachy.


It should feel like an illustrator noticed something ridiculous but familiar and captured it in a single image.


## FORBIDDEN


* Color of any kind
* Photorealism
* Realistic portraiture
* Naturalistic human anatomy
* Conventional realistic facial proportions
* Digital painting
* Smooth digital shading
* Airbrush effects
* 3D rendering
* Corporate vector illustration
* Stock-art aesthetic
* Perfect geometry
* Perfect symmetry
* Highly polished line art
* Anime or manga styling
* Cute mascot-style cartooning
* Slapstick visual comedy
* Meme-style layouts
* Decorative borders
* Excessive environmental detail
* Heavy rendering
* Clean digital typography dominating the image


## REFERENCE FEELING


Think classic newspaper editorial cartoons and opinion-page caricatures rather than realistic editorial illustration.


Characters should be built from exaggerated shapes, selective distortion, strong silhouettes, and expressive facial features.


The image should feel authored, analog, intelligent, and slightly imperfect.


The goal is not to draw realistic people behaving humorously. The goal is to turn people into expressive caricatures that communicate an idea through economical drawing and visual wit.


## OUTPUT


* 1080x1080px square
* Graphite pencil only
* Cream paper background approximately #F5F5DC
* Visible natural paper grain
* One-panel editorial cartoon
* Clearly caricatured character designs
* Loose hand-drawn execution
* Minimal shading
* Clear visual idea
* Generous negative space
* Analog appearance


Generate the image as though a skilled newspaper cartoonist observed the situation and exaggerated the people, gestures, and proportions into a sharp editorial caricature. Do not include a signature.

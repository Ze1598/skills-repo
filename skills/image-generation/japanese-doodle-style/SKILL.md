Convert the character from the reference image into a Japanese rough digital doodle style.

Overall art style:
An extremely casual, rough, and childlike Japanese moe-style anime character digital doodle, as if quickly drawn using crude crayons or markers from a basic drawing software. Preserve the original character's recognizability, hairstyle, hair color, facial features, clothing, accessories, and signature expression.

Line art:

* Use bold, rough black strokes to outline the character.
* Line thickness is highly uneven, with some parts suddenly thickening and others breaking off.
* Contours are loose, inaccurate, and slightly skewed.
* Lines have a clear hand-drawn grainy and smudged texture.
* No pursuit of precise edging; allow lines to misalign, overlap, and be incomplete.
* Prohibit smooth, refined vector line art.

Coloring:

* Use large areas of opaque, simple flat colors.
* Colors slightly overflow the outlines, allowing for missed spots and white gaps.
* Shadows use minimal rough gray or same-tone darker strokes.
* Highlights use casually smeared short white strokes.
* Retain the rough texture of mixed crayons, pastels, and markers.
* No complex gradients, refined light and shadow, or rich color layering.

Facial features:

* Preserve the original character's eye color, eye shape, and signature demeanor.
* Simplify facial features appropriately; eyes are rendered with thick black lines and simple color blocks.
* Keep the mouth as simple as possible; the nose can be omitted.
* Add minimal casual pink blush strokes to the face.
* Retain the original character's expressive traits without arbitrarily altering their personality or vibe.

Hair and clothing:

* Fully preserve the original character's most recognizable hairstyle and hair color.
* Hair is composed of a few large color blocks; do not draw numerous fine strands.
* Clothing retains the original colors, outlines, and signature accessories.
* Fabric folds and decorations are highly simplified.
* Do not arbitrarily change clothing or add accessories not present in the original image.

Composition:

* 1:1 square canvas.
* Half-body portrait of the character, with the head slightly oversized.
* Character centered, with natural white space preserved.
* Pure white background; no added text, complex scenes, or decorative borders.

Most important style restriction:
The image must be rough, sloppy, simple, and childlike, like a cute anime doodle scribbled out on a whim, not a refined illustration deliberately mimicking a sketch.

Prohibit automatic refinements, prohibit refined line art, prohibit complex hair structures, prohibit subtle gradients, prohibit 3D effects, prohibit excessive light and shadow, prohibit adding complex clothing details.

Final effect: Low completion level, thick black outlines, large flat color blocks, casual white highlights, and clear crayon graininess in a Japanese anime digital doodle style.

---
name: podcast-notebooklm
description: Configure a focused, long-form NotebookLM-style podcast discussion that explains source concepts operationally without intros, outros, filler, interjections, or references to source material. Use when preparing podcast-generation instructions from essays, books, summaries, or related sources.
---

Do not have an intro nor an outro, jump directly to the topic at hand without hesitation.

Do not have pauses nor intermissions nor parts.

Do not use filler words.

Do not include interjections.

Only one speaker at a time.

Discuss the topics of the essay at length.

Discuss all concepts explained throughout the summary.

The goal is for people to have a comprehensive understanding of the skills, concepts, and nuances taken from the book.

Never reference the “the source material” or the author, focus on communicating the ideas.

Never reference a deep dive.

Identify a connecting thread across the sources and build an overview around that thread while naturally building into the ideas of the various sources.

You must bypass generic platitudes in favor of granular, "operational" tactics to break down the mechanics of how things actually work.

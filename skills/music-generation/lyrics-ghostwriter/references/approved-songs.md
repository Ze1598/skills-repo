# User-supplied reference corpus

These are the nine positive references supplied on 2026-10-11 to recalibrate this skill. Preserve their wording; use them as craft references, not a phrase bank. The descriptive headings are reference labels, not invented song titles. Markdown line-break escapes have been removed. Apparent source typos (such as “youx” in song 1), unusual grammar, and spacing are retained; do not imitate them as style rules.

## Index

- [Song 1: Fantasy and longing](#song-1-fantasy-and-longing) — Interleaved imagined and physical worlds; an action-led bridge; diminished feeling in the final chorus.
- [Song 2: Push away, want close](#song-2-push-away-want-close) — Conflicting needs; a good day's commitments become the next day's burden; confession without resolution.
- [Song 3: Usefulness and belonging](#song-3-usefulness-and-belonging) — Sustained lighthouse metaphor; ambiguous evidence; longing to be wanted without providing a service.
- [Song 4: Refusing social performance](#song-4-refusing-social-performance) — Social injury becomes a boundary; speech-led anger; relief rather than compulsory despair.
- [Song 5: Losing resistance](#song-5-losing-resistance) — Panic becomes exhaustion and familiarity; fear of losing fear.
